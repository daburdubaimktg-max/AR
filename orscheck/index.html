    How to spot genuine ORS

    South Africa · Olive Oil range

    • New green & burgundy label. Green top, burgundy band at the bottom.
    • Red ORS logo at the top, above "OLIVE OIL".
    • "OLIVE OIL" is olive green, never black.
    • "Original Formula" in italic script under the product name.
    • No spelling mistakes, and smooth, sharp printing on a sturdy pack.
    • Look-alike brand names such as "MOMOLEMO" on an ORS-style label are fakes.
    • Sizes add up. Fakes often print mismatched sizes, e.g. "420ml 260g (11.7 oz)".

    Buy from trusted stores. If the price is far below normal, be careful.
